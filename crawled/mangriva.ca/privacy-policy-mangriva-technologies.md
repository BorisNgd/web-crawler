# Privacy Policy

We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

Last updated: December 2024

## Data Collection

We only collect data necessary for the proper functioning of our services: • Identification information (name, email, phone) • Connection and usage data • Information about your company • Communications with our team Data is collected with your explicit consent during registration or use of our services.

## Data Usage

Your data is used to: • Provide and improve our AI automation services • Personalize your user experience • Communicate with you about your account • Send information about our products (with your consent) • Ensure the security of our systems • Comply with our legal obligations

## Data Security

We implement robust security measures: • Data encryption in transit and at rest (AES-256) • Two-factor authentication available • Regular security audits • Restricted access to personal data • Continuous threat monitoring • SOC 2 Type II compliance

## Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR and Quebec's Law 25, you have the following rights: • Right to access your personal data • Right to rectify inaccurate information • Right to erasure (right to be forgotten) • Right to data portability • Right to object to processing • Right to withdraw your consent To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@mangriva.ca

## Cookies and Trackers

Our site uses cookies to: • Ensure proper site functionality • Remember your preferences • Analyze site usage (analytics) • Improve our services You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.
